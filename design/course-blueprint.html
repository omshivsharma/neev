Neev. Content prototype v1 · text-first

Learn by the situation you're actually in.

Every lesson opens with a scene from real Indian money life — "₹10,000 aa gaye, ab kya?" — shows the reflex most people follow, then hands you the framework to reason it out. It never tells you where to put the money. It trains you to recognise your own situation and make the call yourself. That's the whole app in one line: situation → the right way to decide.

How every lesson is shaped

The six-beat story

The situationA specific scene you could genuinely be in this week — a bonus lands, a phone cracks, a friend forwards a "tip."
The reflexThe tempting move most people make on autopilot — spend it, panic-sell, chase the tip. Named honestly, without shaming.
The real questionThe one question the scene is actually forcing — almost never "which fund," almost always "when do I need this / can I bear this."
The frameworkThe concept that answers it, taught plainly with one Indian example. Max two new terms, each defined in a sentence.
Your callThe redirect: the right answer depends on your horizon, liquidity need and risk capacity — so here's how to decide, never a named instrument.
Your move todayOne action with virtual money on real market data, plus a reflection question. This is what earns the streak.
The full arc · 7 modules

A learner's journey, not a syllabus

Module 1, written in full

Paisa aaya — ab kya?

Tap any lesson to read the six beats. This is exactly the reading a user sees under the (later) video.

1₹10,000 aaye — kharch, bachao, ya badhao?Lesson 1 · 3 min · the spend / save / invest fork▸
SituationAccount mein achanak ₹10,000 aaye — bonus, gift, ya freelance ka payment. Dil khush. Uparse ek sale ka notification aur ek dost ka message: "yaar isme daal de, badhiya chal raha hai."
The reflexDo common autopilots: (1) turant kharch kar do jab tak hai, ya (2) "invest karna hai" soch ke bina soche kahin bhi daal do — kisi ke kehne pe.
Real questionSabse pehla sawaal "kahan invest karun" nahi hai. Woh hai: is paise ko mujhe kab chahiye? Baaki sab uske baad aata hai.
FrameworkHar rupee ko teen mein se ek dabbe mein socho. (a) Jald zaroorat / emergency — safe aur turant reachable rehna chahiye. (b) 1–3 saal ka goal — thoda alag treatment, bumpiness afford nahi. (c) 5+ saal tak nahi chahiye — yahan "grow karun?" ka sawaal banta hai. Order important hai: cushion pehle, growth baad mein.
Your call₹10,000 kahan jaaye yeh aapki situation pe depend karta hai — aapka existing emergency cushion, agle kuch mahine ki zarooratein, aur horizon. "Kya achha chal raha hai" is the wrong compass. Hum koi jagah name nahi karenge — sirf yeh ki kaise choose karein.
Aaj ka kaamApp mein virtual ₹10,000 ko teen dabbon mein baanto — Jald / Mid / Long — jo ratio aaj sahi lage. App aapke "Jald" dabbe pe ek real savings rate dikhayega.
Emergency cushion: safe, reachable money kept only for the unexpected.Time horizon: how long until you actually need a given rupee.
How tax touches this: savings interest is taxable income in India, with a limited deduction possible — a detail, not a reason to act today. (verify current rate before relying on this)
2Jab zindagi curveball fenkeLesson 2 · 3 min · why emergency fund comes first▸
SituationSaturday raat. Phone haath se gira, screen chaknachoor — ya ghar mein achanak medical bill. ₹15,000 chahiye, abhi.
The reflexYa toh invested paisa nikaalo (jo shayad us din neeche ho), ya credit card / udhaar pe chala jao — dono mehnge pad sakte hain.
Real questionKya aapke paas aisa paisa hai jo bina loss, bina paperwork, bina wait — aaj mil jaaye?
FrameworkEmergency fund = kuch mahine ke essential kharche, alag rakhe hue, safe aur liquid, aur kisi aur cheez ke liye touch nahi. Yeh growth ke liye nahi — peace ke liye hai. Isiliye investing iske baad shuru hoti hai: warna pehla jhatka aapko galat waqt pe bechne pe majboor karega.
Your callKitne mahine ka cushion — 3, 6, ya zyada — yeh aapki income kitni stable hai, kitne log aap pe depend karte hain, aur health pe depend karta hai. Aapki call.
Aaj ka kaamApna asli monthly essential kharcha estimate karke app mein daalo. App 3 aur 6 mahine ka cushion size karega; virtual paise se woh dabba bharo.
Emergency fund: a safe, liquid pot sized in months of essential spending.Liquidity: how fast you can reach cash without a loss.
3Drawer mein pada paisa chhota ho raha haiLesson 3 · 3 min · inflation, the silent shrink▸
SituationDadi ne ₹50,000 ghar pe rakhe — "safe" samajh ke — 5 saal se. Note ginti wahi hai. Unhe lagta hai paisa bacha hua hai.
The reflexYeh maan lena ki idle cash = safe cash, aur untouched paisa apne aap surakshit hai.
Real question5 saal baad us ₹50,000 se aap utna hi khareed paoge jitna aaj?
FrameworkInflation — daam dheere-dheere upar jaate hain, toh same rupees se kam milta hai. Idle "safe" paisa chup-chaap apni buying power khota hai. Volatility se safety aur inflation se safety — do alag cheezein hain.
Your callEmergency ke liye idle cash sahi hai. Par barso tak idle pada long-term paisa ek quiet cost carry karta hai. Kitna idle rakhna — zaroorat se decide karo, dar se nahi.
Aaj ka kaamApp mein virtual ₹50,000 pe latest real inflation figure apply karke 5 saal aage ki buying power dekho.
Inflation: the general rise in prices, so the same rupees buy a little less each year.Purchasing power: what your money can actually buy, not the rupee count.
4₹200 ko ₹400 kaun banata hai — aap nahi, timeLesson 4 · 3 min · compounding, back-loaded▸
SituationDo dost. Ek ne 22 ki umar se chhota-chhota daalna shuru kiya, phir bhool gaya. Doosre ne 32 se double amount daala, disciplined ho ke.
The reflex"Abhi toh kam kamaata hoon — baad mein zyada kama ke, ek saath bada invest kar dunga."
Real questionAage kaun nikla — jisne zyada daala, ya jisne jaldi shuru kiya?
FrameworkCompounding — return on return. Shuru mein flat lagta hai, asli kamaal baad ke saalon mein (back-loaded). Heavy lifting time karta hai, amount nahi. Jaldi shuru = zyada cycles of returns-on-returns.
Your callJo abhi de sakte ho, usse aaj shuru karo — chhota bhi chalega. Kahan point karna hai woh alag, baad ka decision hai (horizon, liquidity, risk pe).
Aaj ka kaamSimulator mein ₹500 ko 5, 15 aur 25 saal aage chala ke woh saal dhoondo jahan curve flat se steep hota hai.
Compounding: earning returns on both your money and the returns it already earned.Time horizon: how long the money stays invested before you need it.
How tax touches this: frequent buying & selling triggers taxes/charges that interrupt compounding; in India equity held over 1 year is taxed differently from under 1 year. (verify current rate before relying on this)
5Shaadi 8 mahine mein, retirement 25 saal doorLesson 5 · 3 min · time horizon decides treatment▸
SituationAapke paas do goals hain, dono ke liye ₹1 lakh: ek behen ki shaadi 8 mahine mein, doosra apni retirement 25 saal door.
The reflexDono ₹1 lakh ko ek jaisa treat karna — same jagah, same soch — kyunki amount same hai.
Real questionKya near-term aur far-term paisa ek jagah, ek tarah hona chahiye — sirf isliye ki number same hai?
FrameworkTime horizon treatment decide karta hai. Short → safe & reachable (bumpiness galat waqt pe chot degi). Long → bumps ride kar sakta hai, time hai recover aur compound hone ka. Isko goal bucketing kehte hain.
Your callHar goal ko uski date ke hisaab se bucket karo; har bucket kahan baithe woh uske horizon + liquidity + risk se decide hota hai — aapke haath mein.
Aaj ka kaamApne asli goals dates ke saath likho; app aaj ki date se horizon nikaalega; virtual paise ko "under 3 years" aur "over 7 years" buckets mein daalo.
Time horizon: years until you actually need a specific pot.Goal bucketing: separating money by goal, each with its own horizon.
6"Yeh double kar dega" — ab kya?Lesson 6 · 3 min · the risk-return smell test▸
SituationWhatsApp pe ek forward — ya ek dost confidently bolta hai: "isme daal, 6 mahine mein double, risk zero, guaranteed." Aur "jaldi karo, offer close ho raha hai."
The reflexFOMO — "sab kama rahe honge, main peeche na reh jaun" — aur bina soche kood jaana.
Real questionHigh return + zero risk + urgency — kya yeh combination real duniya mein possible hai?
FrameworkRisk–return trade-off: jahan zyada return ka mauka, wahan zyada ups-and-downs. "Guaranteed high, zero risk" ek classic red flag hai — aur urgency aapko sochne se rokne ke liye lagayi jaati hai. Yeh sabak aage ke risk module ka darwaza hai.
Your callRuko. Kisi bhi cheez ko apne horizon, risk capacity aur diversification ke lens se judge karo — tip ke basis pe nahi, aur urgency pe kabhi nahi.
Aaj ka kaamApp mein ek broad index ki pichhle 1 saal ki bumpy chaal ko ek fixed rate ke saath rakh ke dekho (real data) — trade-off ko aankhon se mehsoos karo.
Risk-return trade-off: higher potential return almost always comes with more ups and downs.Red flag: a promise of high return with no risk and time pressure — treat with suspicion.
